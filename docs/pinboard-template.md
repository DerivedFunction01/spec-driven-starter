# Inspiration pin board — template

A project's pins: related apps, competitors, and references
kept as a spec artifact (intake-gated: `docs/method.md`).
Inspiration is evidence with a verdict, not a mood. Copy this
skeleton; one row per pin, with its capture kept beside the
board.

```text
# Pin board — <project>

| Pin | Reference | Captured | Take | Do not take | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <short name> | <app / competitor / reference + link> | <date; capture stored at> | <the pattern, flow, feel, or pricing shape — named exactly> | <what is explicitly not borrowed> | <ruling it produced / exception / dropped, with the date> |
```

## Pin discipline

- Pins are made freely during spec and study work; a pin is
  cheap. What is not free is building from one: every pin
  resolves to a ruling, an exception, or a drop *before* its
  surface is built, and the verdict column is where that
  resolution lives.
- "Take" names the transferable pattern, never the asset:
  inspiration never licenses copying assets, copy, or branding
  (imagery discipline, `docs/fundamentals.md`; the mockup
  standard, `docs/mockup-approval.md`).
- Captures are dated because references move; a pin whose
  capture no longer matches its reference is refreshed or
  dropped, never silently stale.
- Pins refresh at a redesign and at conversion intake; the
  refresh re-rules or drops every open pin.
