# §0 Product summary (sample)

A small club lends field kits — named sets of gear such as a
River Day Kit or a Trail Repair Kit. Members need one answer at
a glance: is this kit ready to take out, running low, or missing
items? The service tracks kits and item counts, derives
readiness on the backend, and serves one display-ready model to
a simple client. Sample data is fiction, seeded in
`backend/infra/sample_data.py`.
